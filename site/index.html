Weekly Business Review for Shopify GitHub

Your Shopify weekly business review, in seconds.

Drop in your orders export. Get revenue, orders, AOV, refunds, top products and ROAS with week-over-week changes and a written summary. Free, no sign-up, no API keys.

Upload Shopify orders export (CSV)
Drag and drop it here, or click to choose a file

1 · Export your orders

In Shopify admin go to Orders → Export. Pick a date range covering at least the last two weeks (8 weeks gives a full trend), choose CSV for Excel, Numbers, or other spreadsheet programs, and click Export orders. Shopify emails you the file.

2 · Drop it above

That's it. No login, no app install and no tokens. The report covers the last complete Monday–Sunday week, and you can switch weeks after it loads.

3 · Share or save

Click Download PDF to save it or send it to your team. Want ROAS too? Add an ad spend CSV with date,channel,spend columns (template).

🔒 Private by design. Your file is read and processed entirely in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Close the tab and it's gone.

Optional: AI-written summary · uses your own OpenAI or Anthropic key

The written summary above is generated by rules and needs no key. If you want an AI version, paste your own API key. It stays in this tab's memory only (it isn't saved anywhere), and only the aggregated numbers on this page are sent to the provider: no customer emails or order rows.
